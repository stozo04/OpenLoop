## Policy gate

Official current Google Play guidance:

- Feature graphic: exactly 1024×500.
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG; no alpha.
- Keep critical content central and make edge content expendable.
- Minimize text.
- Avoid price/promotional claims, rankings, awards, badges, testimonials, or unsupported claims.
- Google strongly recommends avoiding device imagery in feature graphics.

Policy critic ruling: remove `Free`, `No Ads`, and `Ad-Free` from the feature graphic. Although truthful, these are treated as price/deal-style promotional metadata and can reduce eligibility for promotion. The Generation 3 finalists comply with that gate. The fact remains available in the short/full listing copy and Play Console declarations.

Policy decision

Google’s current feature-graphic guidance discourages price/promotional language and recommends avoiding device imagery. The independent policy critic therefore rejected “Free” and “Ad-Free” inside the feature graphic even though both claims are truthful. Generation 3 prioritizes promotion eligibility and keeps the business-model promise in the listing copy and Play Console declarations.

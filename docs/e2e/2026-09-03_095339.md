## Findings & issues to research deeper

1. **Reverse preview timed out** — the freshly launched app flow stalled in reverse pass 1 for the full two-minute budget. The app degraded to Forward and remained usable, but the selected reverse mode was not available. Next step: reproduce on a cold-booted emulator with `dumpsys media.codec` captured during the stall and compare against the pixel-sweep fixture.
2. **Codec churn was elevated** — 42 reclaim-pressure, 24 dead-thread, and 47 starvation signatures accompanied only 26 created codec components. This is advisory, but the cluster is consistent with codec teardown/reclaim contention around the stalled reverse.
3. **Scanner timeout over-count** — the generic `Timed out after` signature counted unrelated system logs. Treat the table's 13 as a raw grep count; the app-specific terminal-event count is one.
